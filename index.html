Grandma's Fluffy Pancakes

This recipe is guaranteed to make your pancakes incredibly fluffy.

Prep Time Cook Time Servings
10 mins 15 mins 4 people

Ingredients List:

  • 2 cups of flour
  • 2 eggs
  • Milk (until right consistency)

Steps

  1. Whisk the dry ingredients together.
  2. Add eggs and milk, stir gently.
  3. Cook on a hot griddle.
